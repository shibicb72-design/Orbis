**App name** (30): Orbis Browser

**Full description** (4000):

Orbis is a fast, private web browser for Android, built on GeckoView, the engine of Firefox. It is open source and
has no ads, no account and no tracking.

Privacy
- Enhanced Tracking Protection (Strict) on every tab, with a per-site switch
- Optional uBlock Origin ad blocker, installed from Mozilla Add-ons with your OK
- HTTPS-only mode
- Private tabs that leave no history, cookies or downloads list behind
- Site settings: camera, microphone, location, JavaScript and data, per site

Everyday browsing
- Tabs with page previews, undo close and recently closed tabs
- Search suggestions from your search engine (DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Brave or Startpage), voice search
- Bookmarks, history, downloads, start page with top sites
- Reader view, page translation on your phone, find in page, print or save as PDF
- Saved passwords, locked with your screen lock, with a strong password generator
- Picture-in-picture video and background audio
- Dark theme and dark websites, text size, zoom on any page
- QR code scanner, home screen shortcuts and search widget
- Opens links from other apps in a quick in-app panel

Made for big screens too: a tab strip, split screen and drag and drop on foldables and tablets.

Source code: https://github.com/shibicb72-design/Orbis

Requires Android 12 or newer (minSdk 31).
