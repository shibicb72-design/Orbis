Orbis Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026. Developer: Shibi Babu. App: Orbis (com.shibi.orbis).

Summary

Orbis is a web browser built on Mozilla GeckoView. The developer of Orbis receives no data about you or your browsing.

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting sent to the developer.
  • Your browsing data stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your tabs, history, bookmarks, downloads list, site settings and permission answers, saved passwords and preferences are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Saved passwords are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and viewing them asks for your screen lock. Android backup is turned off for Orbis, so this data is not copied to Google cloud backup. Private tabs keep no history, cookies or downloads list after they close. Errors are written to an on-device error log that you can view in Settings; it is never sent automatically.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Websites you visit receive what any browser sends them, such as your IP address, the page request and cookies they set. Their own privacy policies apply. Orbis turns on Enhanced Tracking Protection (Strict) by default.
  • Search goes to the search engine you chose (DuckDuckGo by default; Google, Bing, Brave or Startpage if you pick them).
  • Search suggestions (on by default, can be turned off in Settings, never in private tabs): what you type in the address bar is sent to your chosen search engine as you type.
  • uBlock Origin (optional, offered on first run): if you choose to install it, Orbis downloads it from Mozilla Add-ons (addons.mozilla.org) and Mozilla checks for its updates. uBlock Origin then downloads its own filter lists from their publishers. uBlock Origin is a separate project; it is not included in Orbis.
  • Safe Browsing: the Gecko engine checks pages against Google Safe Browsing lists to warn you about dangerous sites.
  • Page translation runs on your phone. The language models it needs are downloaded from Mozilla the first time.
  • Voice search uses your phone's speech recognizer (for example Google's). Its own privacy terms apply.
  • Update check: only in copies of Orbis that were not installed from Google Play, and it can be turned off. Once a day Orbis asks GitHub for the latest release number. Nothing else is sent. Copies installed from Google Play do not do this.
  • Cast: when you choose Cast, the video or audio address (and, for YouTube, the video id) is sent only to the TV or speaker you pick on your local network. Orbis sends nothing to Google or any server for Cast. Casting YouTube opens it in the TV's YouTube app, which can show ads and is subject to YouTube's own terms on the TV.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load web pages.
  • Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast and, on Android 13 and later, nearby Wi-Fi devices: to find Chromecast and DLNA speakers and TVs on your local network when you use Cast. Nearby Wi-Fi is not used for location. Orbis does not scan Wi-Fi networks for Cast, and the device list stays on your phone.
  • Camera and microphone: only when a website asks and you allow it, and the camera for the QR code scanner, which reads codes on your phone.
  • Location (approximate and precise): only when a website asks and you allow it. Orbis never uses location in the background.
  • Notifications, foreground service (data sync and media playback) and wake lock: to keep downloads running and to show media controls while page audio or video plays in the background.
  • Biometrics: to unlock saved passwords with your screen lock or fingerprint. Your fingerprint or face data stays with Android.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Clear browsing data in Orbis, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Each removes the data above from your phone. The developer holds no copy.

Children

Orbis is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
