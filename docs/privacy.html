Orbis Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Developer: Shibi Babu. App: Orbis (com.shibi.orbis).

Summary

Orbis is a web browser built on Mozilla GeckoView. The developer of Orbis receives no data about you or your browsing.

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting sent to the developer.
  • Your browsing data stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your tabs, history, bookmarks, downloads list, site settings and permission answers, saved passwords and preferences are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Saved passwords are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and viewing them asks for your screen lock. Android backup is turned off for Orbis, so this data is not copied to Google cloud backup. Private tabs keep no history, cookies or downloads list after they close, and files downloaded in a private tab (file downloads and torrents) are deleted then. Only a torrent you saved with "Save to Downloads" first stays. Errors are written to an on-device error log that you can view in Settings; it is never sent automatically.

The estimated time saved from YouTube ad breaks is stored only on this phone. It records a video id and, when YouTube included one, the title, for a video whose player response scheduled an ad break or whose ad-break request was blocked. Shorts reel ads are not counted. The start page calls this “ad breaks avoided (est.)”. It is not added to the separate per-request time estimate. Private tabs are not included. Nothing about it is sent off the phone.

Tab groups, the order of the menu and the toolbar button you chose, the theme (including Material You, which takes its colours from your wallpaper), search engines you add, and the secure DNS, clear on exit and private-tab lock settings are also stored only on your phone. Clear on exit and the private-tab lock are local: nothing is sent anywhere. Clear on exit wipes your open tabs, history, cookies, site data and cache the next time Orbis starts, not when it closes. The private-tab lock asks for your fingerprint or screen lock when you come back to Orbis; your fingerprint or face data stays with Android. Orbis is available in English only.

What leaves your phone, and when

  • Websites you visit receive what any browser sends them, such as your IP address, the page request and cookies they set. Their own privacy policies apply. Orbis turns on Enhanced Tracking Protection (Strict) by default.
  • Search goes to the search engine you chose (DuckDuckGo by default; Google, Bing, Brave or Startpage if you pick them). If you add a search engine of your own, it receives what you search for from the address bar. Orbis does not ask an engine you added for suggestions.
  • Secure DNS (optional, off by default): when you turn it on, Orbis sends the names of the sites you open, encrypted, to the provider you pick: Quad9 (dns.quad9.net), Cloudflare (cloudflare-dns.com), Mullvad (dns.mullvad.net) or an address you type. That provider's privacy policy applies. If the provider cannot be reached, Orbis uses your phone's DNS. Orbis sends nothing to its developers. When it is off, Orbis uses your phone's DNS.
  • Search suggestions (on by default, can be turned off in Settings, never in private tabs): what you type in the address bar is sent to your chosen search engine as you type.
  • uBlock Origin (optional, offered on first run): if you choose to install it, Orbis downloads it from Mozilla Add-ons (addons.mozilla.org) and Mozilla checks for its updates. uBlock Origin then downloads its own filter lists from their publishers. uBlock Origin is a separate project; it is not included in Orbis.
  • Add-ons (optional, none installed at first): Orbis offers five add-ons: SponsorBlock, Consent-O-Matic, ClearURLs, Dark Reader and Violentmonkey. Each is downloaded from Mozilla's add-on store (addons.mozilla.org) when you add it, and updated from there. They are made by other people, not by Orbis. An add-on can see the pages it has access to; the Add-ons list shows that access before you add it. SponsorBlock contacts sponsor.ajay.app to look up videos. Other add-ons may also contact their own services. Each add-on's own privacy policy applies. An add-on's button or panel can open one of that add-on's own pages in a new tab; it cannot open other addresses that way.
  • Safe Browsing: the Gecko engine checks pages against Google Safe Browsing lists to warn you about dangerous sites.
  • Page translation runs on your phone. The language models it needs are downloaded from Mozilla the first time.
  • Voice search uses your phone's speech recognizer (for example Google's). Its own privacy terms apply.
  • Updates are delivered by Google Play.
  • Cast: when you choose Cast, the video or audio address (and, for YouTube, the video id) is sent only to the TV or speaker you pick on your local network. Orbis sends nothing to Google or any server for Cast. Casting YouTube opens it in the TV's YouTube app, which can show ads and is subject to YouTube's own terms on the TV.

Backup

Backup is optional and only runs when you use Settings > Backup and restore. Orbis saves one file where you choose. It holds your bookmarks (with folders), saved passwords, a fixed list of settings (including the torrent settings), site permission answers you saved, and which add-ons are installed (not their data). Open tabs (addresses and titles) are included if you leave "Include open tabs" ticked, and browsing history only if you tick it. The file is encrypted with a password you type. It does not hold cookies or sign-ins, site data, downloads, private tabs, add-on data or the sites where you turned tracking protection off. Orbis sends the file nowhere and keeps no copy of it or of the password; if you forget the password, the file cannot be opened. If you turn on fingerprint unlock for backups, the password is kept on your phone only, sealed with a key in the Android Keystore that needs your fingerprint. Restoring adds bookmarks and passwords to what is on the phone, replaces the settings in the file, and never installs add-ons.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load web pages.
  • Wi-Fi state, Wi-Fi multicast and, on Android 13 and later, nearby Wi-Fi devices: to find Chromecast and DLNA speakers and TVs on your local network when you use Cast. Nearby Wi-Fi is not used for location. Orbis does not scan Wi-Fi networks for Cast, and the device list stays on your phone.
  • Camera and microphone: only when a website asks and you allow it, and the camera for the QR code scanner, which reads codes on your phone.
  • Location (approximate and precise): only when a website asks and you allow it. Orbis never uses location in the background.
  • Notifications, foreground service (data sync and media playback) and wake lock: to keep downloads running and to show media controls while page audio or video plays in the background.
  • Biometrics: to unlock saved passwords, backups and private tabs with your screen lock or fingerprint. Your fingerprint or face data stays with Android.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Clear browsing data in Orbis, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing browsing history also deletes the YouTube ad-break estimate. Clear on exit (Settings) does the same for open tabs, history, cookies, site data and cache at the next start. Each of these removes the data above from your phone. The developer holds no copy.

Children

Orbis is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children.
